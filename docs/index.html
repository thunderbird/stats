About this data ↗

Active daily installations (ADI) are estimated from Thunderbird application and add-on update requests. They are not a count of individual people. Channel names come from update requests; records without a reported channel appear as Unclassified. Missing dates are gaps, not zero activity. Hiding a series does not change the percentage denominator.
